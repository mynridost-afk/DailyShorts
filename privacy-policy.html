Privacy Policy

Daily Shorts — Last updated: March 2026

1. Overview

Daily Shorts ("the App") is a personal desktop automation tool. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

2. Data We Access

To function, the App may access the following:

  • TikTok account data: Only the permissions required to upload and publish videos on your behalf (video.upload, video.publish). The App does not access your followers, messages, likes, or any other profile data.
  • OAuth tokens: An access token and refresh token are stored locally on your machine (in tiktok_token.json) to avoid requiring login on every run. These are never transmitted to any server other than TikTok's own OAuth endpoints.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit your personal information to any third party
  • Use analytics or tracking
  • Store your TikTok credentials (username or password)
  • Share any data with the App's developers

4. Local Storage

All data (OAuth tokens, generated videos, scripts) is stored locally on your own machine. No data is uploaded to external servers operated by this App.

5. Third-Party Services

The App sends requests to the following third-party APIs as part of normal operation:

  • TikTok API — to publish videos
  • YouTube Data API — to publish videos
  • ElevenLabs API — to generate narration audio
  • fal.ai API — to generate AI video clips
  • DeepSeek API — to generate video scripts

Each service's own privacy policy governs how they handle the data you send to them.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please open an issue on the project repository.
